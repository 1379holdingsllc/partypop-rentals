How it works

Three simple steps. Most requests take about two minutes.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Pick a service, add a few details and choose a preferred date.

  2. We confirm details & price

    We review your request and follow up by phone or text with a price.

  3. Get it done

    Once you confirm, pay by PayPal invoice. Providers are paid after the job or rental is completed.

A submitted request is not a confirmed booking. We confirm details and price with you first.

Questions? Quick answers.

What happens after I send a request?

A real person reviews it and follows up by phone or text, usually the same day, to ask any questions and confirm the details and price.

Is my request a confirmed booking?

No. Sending a request is free and doesn't book or charge anything. Nothing is booked until we confirm the details and price with you and you say yes.

What details should I include?

Your event date, the items you need, your guest count, and where it's going (backyard, park, hall). For tools, tell us how long you need it and what the job is.

Is delivery and setup included?

It depends on the item and the provider. Tell us if you need delivery, setup or pickup, and we'll confirm what's included and the price before you book.

I have party gear or tools to rent out. How do I list them?

Use the “List your party gear” or “List your tools” form on this page. Add your rates and a link to photos if you have them. We review every listing before it goes live.

How do I pay?

There's no charge to send a request. Once you confirm a booking, you pay by PayPal invoice. Providers are paid after the job or rental is completed.

Can I change or cancel my request?

Before you confirm, nothing is booked, so you can change or cancel anytime. Just call or text (470) 279-6694. After you confirm, let us know as early as you can.
